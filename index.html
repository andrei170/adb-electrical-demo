Solar & battery

MCS certified — which is what lets you claim the export payments

We are registered with MCS for both solar PV and battery storage under TrustMark licence 1852019. Plenty of firms will fit panels. Fewer can certify them.

Why MCS matters

It is not a badge, it is the paperwork

  • The Smart Export Guarantee requires an MCS-certified installation — without it, your energy supplier will not pay you for what you export
  • Grant and incentive schemes are written around MCS certification
  • Your certificate is registered, so it stays valid if you sell the house
  • We certify both the panels and the battery, not just one of them
How we size it

Against your bill, not your roof

  • We look at what you actually use, and when you use it
  • Battery sized to carry the evening, not to fill the garage
  • Existing supply and earthing checked before anything is quoted
  • The whole installation certified and notified as one job
Media walls

The part nobody sees is the part that matters.

A media wall lives or dies on what happens before the plasterboard goes on — where the power sits, how the data runs, and whether the heat has anywhere to go.

  • Sockets and data positioned before the frame goes up
  • Ventilation planned for the equipment, not added afterwards
  • Feature and ambient lighting on their own switching
  • We work alongside your joiner and plasterer, not after them
